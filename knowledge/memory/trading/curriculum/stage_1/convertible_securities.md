**Trading implications:**
- Use trailing P/E for mature companies with stable earnings history
- Use forward P/E for growth assessment and future profitability evaluation
- Enter positions on confirmed breakouts
- Monitor convertible securities for arbitrage opportunities between bond price and underlying stock
- Consider convertible securities for downside protection with upside participation in volatile markets
- Evaluate conversion triggers and call provisions when timing convertible positions
- Assess diluted EPS impact when analyzing companies with significant convertible securities outstanding

**Risk factors:**
- Interest rate risk affecting convertible bond values
- Credit risk of issuer default
- Liquidity risk due to lower trading volumes in convertible securities
- Call provision risk limiting upside potential through forced conversion
- Opportunity cost of lower yield versus non-convertible bonds
- Dilution risk to existing shareholders upon conversion
- Complex valuation requiring specialized analysis
- Market conversion price may never be reached, leaving investor with suboptimal returns
