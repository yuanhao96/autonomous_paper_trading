## 2026-03-11 — Technical Analysis - The Complete Resource for Financial Market Technicians 2nd edition 2010 — part 3

The documents synthesize technical analysis methodologies centered on trend identification, support/resistance levels, and breakout trading strategies. Key themes include distinguishing true breakouts from false breakouts (fakeouts) through volume confirmation and indicator validation, applying Dow Theory principles for trend classification, and integrating risk management techniques including stop-loss placement and position sizing. The materials emphasize that successful breakout trading requires multi-timeframe analysis, patience for confirmation, and awareness of psychological factors like FOMO that drive false signals.

**Key concepts:** Support and Resistance, Breakout, False Breakout/Fakeout, Dow Theory, Trend Analysis, Volume Confirmation, Trailing P/E Ratio, Forward P/E Ratio, TTM/LTM Earnings, Technical Indicators (MACD, RSI, ADX, ATR, VWAP, OBV), Retracement, Pullback, Throwback, Point-and-Figure Charts, Market Efficiency Hypothesis, Behavioral Finance

**Trading implications:**
- Wait for volume confirmation (2-3x average) before entering breakout trades
- Use multiple timeframe analysis to filter false signals
- Place stops 2-3% below entry for breakouts or use ATR-based stops
- Wait for retest of broken level before entry to reduce fakeout risk
- Risk only 1-3% of account per trade with proper position sizing
- Use MACD, RSI, ADX, and ATR as false breakout filters
- Enter against breakouts when clear fakeout signals present for high win-rate strategy
- Scale into positions using 2-3 entry points
- Set profit targets at prior swing highs/lows or use 2:1+ risk-reward ratios
- Use trailing P/E for mature companies and forward P/E for growth assessment

**Risk factors:**
- False breakouts/fakeouts trapping traders with FOMO-driven entries
- Low liquidity causing failed breakouts without follow-through
- Whipsaws from entering before confirmation
- Psychological pressure to chase breakouts immediately
- News events and earnings causing unexpected volatility
- Over-leverage in options/futures leading to losses beyond initial investment
- ADX below 20 indicating weak trend strength and higher breakout failure rates
- Contracting ATR before breakouts signaling low volatility and potential failure
- Failed retests of broken levels invalidating trade thesis
