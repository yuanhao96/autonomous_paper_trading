## 2026-03-08 — knowledge/memory/trading/curriculum/stage_1/transaction_risk.md

Foreign exchange risk encompasses three main types (transaction, translation, and economic risk) arising from currency fluctuations, with hedging strategies including forwards, options, and natural hedging essential for mitigation. Currency diversification serves as protection against inflation and government debt devaluation, while forex markets provide early warning signals and intermarket analysis opportunities. The carry trade strategy exploits interest rate differentials but carries significant unwinding risks.

**Key concepts:** Foreign exchange risk (FX risk), Transaction risk, Translation risk, Economic risk (forecast risk), Contingent risk, Currency diversification, Carry trade, Hedging, Forward contracts, Options, Natural hedging, Value at risk (VaR), Intermarket analysis, Trading sessions (Tokyo/London/New York), Functional currency, Balance sheet hedge, Forward premium puzzle, Liquidity black holes, Carry trade unwinding

**Trading implications:**
- Maintain multi-currency exposure to hedge against government-induced inflation and debt devaluation
- Trade during London-New York session overlap for peak liquidity and volatility
- Use forex market movements as early warning signals for other asset classes
- Exploit interest rate differentials through carry trades while monitoring unwind risks
- Implement hedging strategies using forwards, options, or natural hedging for FX exposure
- Apply intermarket analysis across stocks, bonds, commodities, and currencies to exploit correlation shifts

**Risk factors:**
- Sudden carry trade unwinding causing global market volatility (demonstrated by 2024 yen carry trade collapse)
- Liquidity black holes during market stress
- Government-induced inflation and debt devaluation
- Exchange rate fluctuations affecting transaction settlement values
- Macroeconomic conditions impacting economic risk (geopolitical instability, government regulations)
- Translation risk affecting reported earnings and stock price
- Contingent risk from pending contracts or negotiations
- Inefficient foreign exchange markets deviating from purchasing power parity or interest rate parity
